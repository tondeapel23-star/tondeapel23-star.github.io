Privacy Policy — Block Quest: Brain Adventure

Application: Block Quest: Brain Adventure (com.playandroid.blockquest)
Effective Date: November 20, 2026
Contact Email: tondeapel23@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Quest: Brain Adventure handles information when you play our puzzle game on Android devices. We respect your privacy and collect no personal data directly.

1. No Account & No Personal Data Collected Directly

Block Quest: Brain Adventure does not require accounts, registration, or login. We do not collect, access, or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera, photos, or precise GPS location. All portrait puzzles use original built-in stylized illustrations — no user photos or face recognition are ever used.

2. Local Game Progress & Statistics Storage

Your unlocked levels (500 Adventure levels), stars, XP level, coins, power-ups, high scores across all game modes, unlocked portraits, unlocked visual themes, daily streak calendar, achievements, trophies, play time, and settings are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. Uninstalling the app removes local data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Consent (GDPR / UMP)

Block Quest: Brain Adventure is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob (discreet banners in menus only, occasional interstitial ads at natural level transitions, and optional user-initiated rewarded video ads).

  • Google AdMob may automatically process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic telemetry to serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements.
  • For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent prior to serving personalized ads.
  • You can review or change your ad privacy preferences at any time inside the game via Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent (GDPR), or through your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • For more information, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

All network traffic initiated by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Game progress remains strictly on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Block Quest: Brain Adventure is designed for a general audience (13+) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or legal requirements. Changes will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
