Privacy Policy

Radio Asia Live • Android Application
Last Updated: November 19, 2026 • Effective Date: Permanent

This Privacy Policy explains how Radio Asia Live ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy.

Summary: Radio Asia Live does NOT require you to register, log in, or provide personal details such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings. We do not track or store your personal identity.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in strict data minimization:

  • No Account Required: You can browse and listen to over 2,300+ Asian radio stations anonymously without creating an account.
  • No Microphone Access: The app only plays outgoing audio streams; it never listens, records, or accesses your device microphone.
  • No GPS Location Tracking: The app does not request or track fine or coarse GPS coordinates. Station search by country/region is performed offline via local application data.
  • No Contact or Media Library Access: We do not read your address book, files, SMS, camera, or private media.

2. Information Automatically Processed

To ensure audio streaming reliability, crash diagnostics, and ad delivery, the following non-personally identifiable technical information may be automatically processed:

  • Device Model & Operating System: Android version, device manufacturer, screen resolution, and audio hardware capabilities to optimize Equalizer, 3D Surround, and DSP playback.
  • Advertising Identifier (AAID): Google Advertising ID used solely by Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements, subject to your personalized ad preferences.
  • App Performance & Crash Data: Anonymous diagnostic telemetry provided by Google Play Services to identify and fix technical glitches.
  • Local App Preferences: Your list of Favorite stations, Recently Played stations, Equalizer presets, and Sleep Timer settings are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Shared Preferences. This data never leaves your device.

3. Android Permissions Explained

Radio Asia Live requests only the minimal set of permissions required for an exceptional streaming radio experience:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to public Asian radio webcast servers and buffer audio streams.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android 14+ / Android 16 to keep the music playing smoothly in the background when you turn off your screen or use other apps.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to display a rich playback notification with station title, artist information, Play/Pause, and Stop buttons on your lockscreen and status bar.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents system CPU sleep from cutting off the live audio stream while you are actively listening.

4. Advertising & AdMob Policy Compliance

We display advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support free continuous broadcasting for all users:

  • Google AdMob Policy Compliance: In accordance with Google Play's Better Ads Standards and Google AdMob Developer Policies, background audio from radio playback is automatically paused whenever a full-screen interstitial advertisement is displayed, ensuring no audio interference or sound clash occurs. Music playback automatically resumes once the ad is closed.
  • User Consent (GDPR / CPRA): For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and relevant regions, we integrate Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management SDK to allow you to customize or withdraw your consent for personalized ads at any time.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application interacts with trusted industry-standard third-party services that have their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Public Radio Webcasts: When connecting to a stream, standard HTTP headers (such as User-Agent) are sent to the originating broadcast server to negotiate codec playback.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Radio Asia Live does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Our content consists of public music, news, and cultural radio streams intended for general audiences. If you believe that a child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

7. Data Security

We prioritize security by utilizing modern Android architectural components, encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections for stream resolution, and ProGuard/R8 byte-code protection. No remote databases hold user records or listening profiles.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
