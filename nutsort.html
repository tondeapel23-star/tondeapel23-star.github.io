Privacy Policy

App Name: Nut Sort: Color Bolt Puzzle (com.nutsort3d.master)
Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nut Sort: Color Bolt Puzzle ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you install and play our Android puzzle game.

1. No Account Required & Local Progress Storage

The App does not require user registration, login, or personal profiles. All game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, daily streaks, unlocked cosmetics, and settings) is stored locally on your device using Android local storage.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free to play, the App integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner ads (on menu screens only), interstitial ads (at natural level breaks), and optional user-initiated rewarded video ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data as required to serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, including:

  • Device or other identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interactions, ad performance metrics, and crash/diagnostic data

You can review Google's advertising privacy practices here: Google Privacy & Terms – Advertising.

3. GDPR, EEA & Consent Management (Google UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and other applicable jurisdictions, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized ads. You can update or withdraw your advertising consent preferences at any time directly inside the App via Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent.

4. Offline Gameplay

Core gameplay works fully offline without an internet connection. No personal gameplay data is transmitted to developer-operated servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security & Deletion

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you can erase all local game data at any time via Settings → Reset Local Progress or by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect SDK updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page for Nut Sort: Color Bolt Puzzle.
