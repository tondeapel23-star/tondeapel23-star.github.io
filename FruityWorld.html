Privacy Policy

App: Fruity World Boom Adventures (com.fruitworld.adventure)
Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fruity World Boom Adventures handles information when you use our Android mobile game. We respect your privacy and collect only what is necessary to provide gameplay, optional leaderboards, and advertising.

1. Local Gameplay Data

Your level progress, stars, virtual coins, settings (language, theme, sound, haptics, reduced animations), and daily challenge history are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. No account is required to play.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob) & GDPR Consent

We use Google AdMob to display optional ads (menu banners, occasional interstitials between levels, and strictly voluntary rewarded video ads). Depending on your region and consent choices collected through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction metrics to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent fraud.

You can review or change your privacy and consent preferences at any time inside the game's Settings screen (where applicable under EEA/UK regulations) or through your device's Google Ads settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy & How Google uses information from apps.

3. Google Play Games Services (Optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services to view or submit scores to the global leaderboard, Google processes your Play Games profile and leaderboard score under the Google Privacy Policy. Signing in is completely optional and the game works offline without it.

4. Virtual Currency

Virtual coins earned in the game have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred outside the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Fruity World Boom Adventures is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because game progress is stored locally on your device, you can delete all local game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a revised effective date.
