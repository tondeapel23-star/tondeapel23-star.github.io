Privacy Policy & Official Data Sources

Application: Smart Finance • Package ID: com.smartfinance.tools • Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Finance ("the App") handles information when you use our European financial calculators, currency converter, and macroeconomic statistics tools on Android. We designed the App with a strict privacy-by-design, local-first architecture.

1. No Account, No Backend Server, No Banking Credentials

  • No Registration Required: You can use all features of Smart Finance without creating an account, signing in, or providing your name, email address, or phone number.
  • No Proprietary Server: We do not operate a backend server and do not collect, store, synchronize, or sell your personal financial calculations or records.
  • No Banking or Wallet Access: The App never requests bank logins, card numbers, IBANs, PINs, or cryptocurrency wallet keys, and never executes financial transactions.

2. Local On-Device Data Storage & User Control

All personal entries you optionally create—such as Debt Payoff plans, Net Worth assets/liabilities, Financial Calendar reminders, FX Rate Alerts, manual Crypto Portfolio entries, and language/country preferences—are stored exclusively locally on your device using Android's sandboxed app storage (DataStore and private app files).

  • Export Backup: You may export your local entries at any time in JSON format from Settings → Export Backup (JSON).
  • Permanent Deletion: You may erase all local user records immediately via Settings → Delete All Local Data, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Offline Functionality: All local calculators, planners, and cached rates continue working offline without an internet connection.

3. Advertising, Analytics & GDPR Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

To keep Smart Finance free without paid subscriptions, the App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • GDPR / EEA / UK / Switzerland Consent: Before requesting ads in regulated regions, the App integrates Google's official User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK so you can grant, customize, or decline consent for personalized advertising.
  • Data Processed by Google AdMob: Depending on your consent choices, Google AdMob may process technical data such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address (for general region/fraud prevention), device type, and ad performance metrics.
  • Google Policies: Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Android Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch public exchange rates, macroeconomic indicators, and serve AdMob ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Used solely to deliver local on-device notifications when an FX rate alert configured by you reaches its target threshold via Android WorkManager.

Smart Finance does not request GPS/location permissions (country selection uses standard device locale or manual user choice), camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or external storage permissions.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR (EEA / UK / Switzerland)

Because Smart Finance does not collect or store your personal financial records on external servers, you retain full direct control over your local data on your device (access, export via JSON backup, and erasure via Settings). For advertising data processed by Google AdMob, you may manage or withdraw your consent at any time via your device's Google Ad Settings or by resetting your Advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

Smart Finance is designed for general adult audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Financial & Tax Disclaimer: All calculations, tax breakdowns, currency conversions, inflation models, and economic charts in Smart Finance are provided strictly for informational and educational purposes. They do not constitute financial, banking, tax, legal, or investment advice, nor binding bank offers. Always verify final figures with an authorized financial institution or certified tax advisor.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, updated official data sources, or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.
