1. Introduction

Welcome to Radio Australia Live ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android application.

Radio Australia Live does NOT collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) directly. We do not require account creation, login credentials, passwords, names, telephone numbers, or physical addresses.

2. Information Collected Automatically & Third-Party SDKs

While using our application, certain technical and non-personal data may be collected automatically by trusted third-party SDKs strictly to deliver app functionality, stream radio stations, and display advertisements:

A. Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK

Our application uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-sensitive data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region, e.g., Australia, to serve relevant local ads).
  • Diagnostic and performance metrics (ad impressions, clicks, load errors).

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

B. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & GDPR/CCPA Consent

We implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with European Union GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) regulations. Users in the EEA, UK, and relevant regions are presented with a consent dialog allowing them to choose personalized or non-personalized ads, or to manage individual vendor consents. You can reopen and adjust these privacy choices at any time from the app's Settings → Privacy & Consent Options.

C. Live Audio Streaming (Icecast / Shoutcast / HLS)

When you select a radio station, our player connects directly to the broadcaster's streaming server. During audio playback, your device transmits standard HTTP network requests containing your IP address to the audio streaming provider so they can deliver the continuous audio stream.

3. Permissions Used by the Application

Radio Australia Live requests only permissions essential for its core functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to connect to live radio audio streams and load the station catalog.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Used to monitor network connectivity and automatically rebuffer streams when switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android 14, 15, and 16 to keep radio playback alive in the background while the screen is locked or while using other apps, displaying a standard playback notification with Play/Pause controls.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the media playback notification.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the device CPU from sleeping during active audio playback.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play Services permission for AdMob ad delivery.

4. Data Retention and Storage

All user preferences (such as your favorite radio stations and recently played history) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

5. Australian Privacy Principles (APP) Compliance

As an Australian-focused service, we adhere to the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). We do not solicit personal information, and any cross-border technical data flow (such as AdMob cloud processing) is conducted in accordance with Google's global data protection standards.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Radio Australia Live is a general audience entertainment application and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Our app employs a Zero-Crash Architecture with robust global crash safeguards to protect system stability and user privacy.
