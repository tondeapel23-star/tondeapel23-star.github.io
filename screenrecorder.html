Privacy Policy — Screen Recorder Pro 1080p

Effective Date: November 2026 (Google Play 2026–2027 Policy Compliant) • Package: com.screenrecorder.pro1080

1. 100% Local On-Device Processing

Screen Recorder Pro 1080p processes and stores all screen recordings, screenshots, front-camera Facecam overlays, custom watermarks, and video edits exclusively on your device. We never upload, transmit, view, or store your videos, audio tracks, images, or camera stream on any external server.

2. Permissions Used & Why

  • Screen Capture (MediaProjection): Starts strictly after your explicit tap and Android system confirmation. A persistent notification is always visible while capturing.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Requested only when you enable microphone or internal audio capture.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Requested only when you explicitly turn on the Facecam feature.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Optional — used only if you enable the Floating Control Bubble or Tutorial Drawing Brush.
  • Photos, Videos & Audio (MediaStore): Used solely to save and manage your own recordings, screenshots, and extracted audio in your device gallery.

3. Security, DRM & Protected Content

This app strictly respects Android FLAG_SECURE and DRM protections. It never performs hidden or background recording without consent, never bypasses protected apps, and never records phone calls.

4. Advertising & User Consent (Google AdMob / UMP)

Screen Recorder Pro 1080p is completely free with no subscriptions and is supported by Google AdMob banner and frequency-capped interstitial ads. Ads are never displayed during active screen recording or video export. Where required (EEA, UK, US states), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects and honors your ad privacy consent, which you can update anytime in Settings.

5. No Accounts, No Cloud & No Third-Party Analytics

We do not require registration or sign-in, and we do not integrate Firebase, Crashlytics, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email tondeapel23@gmail.com listed on the Google Play Store page for Screen Recorder Pro 1080p.
