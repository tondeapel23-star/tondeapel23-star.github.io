Privacy Policy — FoodScan Barcode Pro

Package Name: com.foodscan.barcode.pro  |  Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Welcome to FoodScan Barcode Pro. This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed, which external API and open data sources we use, and your privacy rights.

1. No Account, No Developer Server, Local-First Storage

FoodScan Barcode Pro does not require registration or login. We do not operate any developer backend server and do not collect or sell your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location.

  • Local Storage: Scan history, favorites, comparisons, personal allergen alerts, language, and theme settings are stored exclusively on your device (local SQLite database and Android DataStore).
  • You can clear local history and cache anytime in Settings → Clear History & Cache or by uninstalling the app.

2. Camera & Offline Barcode Scanning (Google ML Kit)

Camera access (android.permission.CAMERA) is requested only when opening the scanner. Barcode recognition (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E) runs 100% locally on your device via CameraX and Google ML Kit. Camera frames and gallery photos are never uploaded to our servers.

3. External Product Data Source & API — Open Food Facts

When looking up a barcode not yet cached locally, the app sends an HTTPS request containing only the product barcode (no personal user data) to the public Open Food Facts API v3:

  • Source & API Endpoint: https://world.openfoodfacts.org (API v3)
  • API Documentation: https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/
  • Database License: Open Database License (ODbL v1.0)
  • Individual Contents License: Database Contents License (DbCL v1.0)
  • Product Photographs: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA 3.0)
  • Open Food Facts Privacy Policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
Informational & Allergen Disclaimer: FoodScan Barcode Pro is an informational tool only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or guarantee allergen safety. Product data comes from the community-maintained Open Food Facts database and may be incomplete. Always check the physical product packaging, especially for severe allergies.

4. Advertising & GDPR Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

FoodScan Barcode Pro displays discreet ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) on non-critical screens (never inside the scanner or over allergen warnings).

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), approximate IP-based location, and ad interaction diagnostics.
  • For EEA, UK, and Swiss users, we integrate Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for GDPR consent. You can update consent anytime via Settings → Privacy Policy & Ad Consent.
  • Google Advertising Privacy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Permissions Used

  • CAMERA: Optional, used solely for local barcode scanning (manual barcode entry is always available).
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to query Open Food Facts API v3 and load Google AdMob ads/consent forms.
