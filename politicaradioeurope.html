Privacy Policy

Dance Radio Europe • Effective Date: November 14, 2026
Key Summary: Dance Radio Europe is 100% free. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, and no subscriptions. All bookmarks (Favorites) and settings remain strictly stored on your own device.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Dance Radio Europe does not require user registration. We do not gather personal identities, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS location, or device documents.

Your user preferences—such as favorite radio stations, recent play history, and sleep timer durations—are saved strictly on your local device via Android SharedPreferences and never transmitted to our servers.

2. Android Permissions Used

The app only requests the minimum permissions mandated by the Android operating system to operate:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: To establish connections with online radio streaming servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To monitor connection stability and ensure smooth playback.
  • android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: To provide background playback and lockscreen audio controls via AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Solely to display player notification controls (Play/Pause/Skip) in your notification tray.
  • android.permission.WAKE_LOCK: To prevent playback interruption when your screen is locked.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dance Radio Europe displays ads powered by Google AdMob to remain completely free for our listeners. Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID, device model, and general geographic location based on IP address).

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and European privacy directives, the app integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). European users can update or revoke their advertising consent choices at any time from the app's Settings > Privacy Options.

Learn more about Google's data handling at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Streaming Radio Content

Dance Radio Europe acts as an audio player directory for publicly accessible shoutcast/icecast/HLS streams provided by third-party radio stations. Broadcasters may collect standard HTTP server logs when your device connects to their audio server. We recommend checking individual broadcaster websites for their specific privacy terms.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
