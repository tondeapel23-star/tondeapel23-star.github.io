USA Radio Live

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Summary: USA Radio Live respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data directly. The application does not require user registration, accounts, phone numbers, or passwords.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application USA Radio Live ("the App"), developed by Luigy. By using USA Radio Live, you agree to the practices described in this document.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. USA Radio Live does not collect:

  • Your personal identity (name, physical address, email, phone number).
  • Your contact list or phonebook.
  • Your personal photos, files, or local media storage.
  • Microphone or camera recordings.

3. Local Storage on Device

The app stores non-personal preferences strictly on your local device (Android DataStore):

  • Favorites: Stations you marked as favorite.
  • Recent History: Stations recently played for quick resumption.
  • App Settings: Sleep timer and audio quality preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is erased when clearing app data or uninstalling.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To support continuous development and maintain free access to radio streaming, USA Radio Live integrates Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-sensitive device telemetry:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) for ad serving and frequency capping.
  • Device model, OS version, and network carrier.
  • Coarse location at country/city level derived from IP address.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices: How Google uses information from apps.

5. Android System Permissions

  • INTERNET: To connect to live online radio streams and load AdMob ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To detect active Wi-Fi or mobile data connections and manage stream reconnection.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Media Playback): To maintain uninterrupted audio playback when the screen is locked or while multitasking.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display playback media controls on lock-screen and status notification bar.
  • WAKE_LOCK: To prevent the device CPU from sleeping while streaming audio.

6. Radio Streams & Broadcasters

USA Radio Live is an index player connecting to public internet radio webcasts broadcast directly by licensed radio stations. Broadcasters may log standard HTTP request metadata according to their individual privacy terms.

7. Children's Privacy

USA Radio Live does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK).
