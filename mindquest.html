# Privacy Policy — MindQuest – Brain & Logic **Effective Date:** November 14, 2026 **Application:** MindQuest – Brain & Logic **Developer:** Luigy (Independent Developer) **Contact:** luigy.developer@gmail.com Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how **MindQuest – Brain & Logic** ("the Application", "we", "our") handles information when you download, install, and enjoy our mobile puzzle game on Android devices. --- ## 1. Executive Summary * **No Personal Data Collection:** MindQuest does not require user accounts, logins, or registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise GPS locations, or contacts. * **100% Offline Gameplay & Local Storage:** All game progression (solved levels, coins, XP, stars, unlocked themes, bookmarks, and cognitive radar statistics) is stored exclusively on your device using Android's private `SharedPreferences` sandbox. * **Offline Backup & Restore (`MQ1-...`):** The progress export and import feature functions entirely offline. Backup strings are computed client-side on your device and are never transmitted to any external cloud database. * **Privacy-Safe Advertising & GDPR Consent (Google AdMob):** We integrate Google AdMob with the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to comply with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, and CCPA standards. --- ## 2. What Information We Handle and How It Is Used ### a) Game Progress Data (Locally Stored on Device) To provide a smooth, persistent gameplay experience across sessions, the following data points are stored **locally within your device's isolated application storage**: * Solved levels across the 4 core categories (Logic & Deduction, Trivia & Knowledge, Riddles & Enigmas, Sort & Organize); * Gold stars, virtual coins, XP points, and available hints; * Active daily streak counter, best streak, and claimed Daily Streak Shields; * Unlocked visual theme IDs and active theme selection; * User preferences (Romanian / English language, procedural sound synthesizer, haptic vibration feedback, large text accessibility). > **Note:** This data never leaves your device and is automatically removed if you uninstall the application or choose the *"Restart Game from Scratch (Reset)"* option in the Settings menu. ### b) Technical Data Processed by Third-Party Services (Google AdMob) The Application utilizes **Google AdMob** (Google LLC) to deliver non-intrusive banner and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain pseudo-anonymized device and diagnostic information, including: * Mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID - GAID); * Device technical characteristics (device model, Android OS version, screen resolution, locale/language settings); * Aggregated ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded video views); * Approximate coarse location (country or city level derived from IP address) to serve regionally appropriate ads. --- ## 3. Advertising Consent & GDPR / UMP Management In accordance with European Union **GDPR** regulations and UK/Swiss privacy laws, users are presented with a consent form powered by Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP). Through this form, and at any time via **Settings -> Manage Ad Privacy Consent (GDPR)**, you can: * Grant consent for personalized advertising; * Opt for non-personalized advertising (contextual ads that do not track user activity across apps); * Review, modify, or revoke your consent choices at any time directly within the game. For detailed information on how Google processes advertising data, please review: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). --- ## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA) MindQuest – Brain & Logic is designed as a family-friendly puzzle experience suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). Advertisements served through Google AdMob adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines and content ratings. --- ## 5. Device Permissions Requested The Application requests only the minimal standard Android permissions necessary for operation: * `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required solely for Google AdMob ad requests and GDPR consent synchronization. * `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to verify internet connectivity before initiating ad requests. * `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback during gameplay actions (can be disabled at any time in Settings). > **Permissions We NEVER Request:** The Application does NOT request access to your Camera, Microphone, Precise GPS Location, Contacts, Call Logs, Photos, or External Shared Storage. --- ## 6. Data Security Because all game progression is stored within the private, sandboxed internal storage of your device, your data is shielded by Android's robust operating system security model. No other applications on your device can access your MindQuest game save data. --- ## 7. User Rights and Controls Under applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA), you retain full sovereignty over your information: * **Right of Erasure:** You can erase all gameplay data and achievements at any time by selecting *"Reset & Restart All Progress"* in Settings or by uninstalling the application. * **Data Portability:** You can export your entire progression at any time as an offline text backup code (`MQ1-...`) and save it in your personal notes. * **Ad Tracking Opt-Out:** You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at the OS level (*Android Settings -> Google -> Ads*). --- ## 8. Updates to this Policy We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated effective date. --- ## 9.
