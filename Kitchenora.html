Privacy Policy — Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  Package: com.cemancamazi.app

Summary: Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry is designed to work 100% offline. No user account or registration is required. Your pantry inventory, custom recipes, favorites, meal plans, and shopping lists are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Kitchenora Studio does not operate backend user servers for Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry and does not collect, sell, or store your personal data on external servers. Specifically, we do not require your name, email address, phone number, location, or account login.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All app data you create stays strictly on your device in local storage (SQLite and Android DataStore):

  • Your pantry inventory (fridge, freezer, pantry, spices, quantities, and optional expiry reminders)
  • Your custom family recipes
  • Your favorite recipes, weekly meal plans, shopping lists, and local cooking history
  • App preferences (language, measurement units, allergen exclusions)

You can export or import a local JSON backup at any time using Android's system file picker, or erase all local app data from Settings → Clear all local data.

3. Advertising & GDPR / UMP Consent (Google AdMob)

To keep Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry free to use, the app displays non-intrusive ads provided by Google AdMob on selected navigation screens (never during active Cooking Mode).

  • Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and diagnostic/usage signals to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.
  • For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request and respect your GDPR consent preferences before requesting personalized ads.
  • Learn more at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for loading AdMob ads and consent forms when online. Core recipe, pantry, meal planning, and cooking features work completely offline.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional): Used only if you enable notifications for cooking step timers.

5. Culinary & Food Safety Disclaimers

Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry is an informational cooking and kitchen organization tool, not a medical, dietary, or food-safety device. Allergen tags are informative only — always verify product labels and cross-contamination risks. User-entered expiry reminders do not certify food freshness or safety.

6. Children's Privacy

Kitchenora Recipes & Pantry is intended for a general audience (adults and families) and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. The updated date will always be shown at the top of this page.
