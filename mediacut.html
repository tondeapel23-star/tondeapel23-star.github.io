This Privacy Policy explains how Media Cut Audio & Video Editor ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and designed the App so that all audio editing, voice recording, video trimming, and wallpaper customization happen 100% locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal audio files, videos, voice recordings, contacts, or wallpapers on any external server. All media processing is performed strictly offline on your device.

2. Permissions Used by the App

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you explicitly choose to record voice clips inside the Audio Studio. Recordings stay locally on your device.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS / WRITE_CONTACTS): Used only when you choose "Assign to Contact" to set a custom ringtone for a specific contact. We never read, upload, or share your contact list.
  • Modify System Settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): Used only when you apply an exported sound as your device's default ringtone, alarm, or notification tone.
  • Set Wallpaper (SET_WALLPAPER): Used only when you apply a built-in wallpaper to your Home or Lock screen.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used only to import audio/video files you select via the Android system picker and to save exported ringtones, MP4 clips, or images to your device's media library.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to load advertisements and manage GDPR/UMP privacy consent preferences via Google AdMob.

3. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads (banner and occasional interstitial ads). Google AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and basic diagnostic/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland are presented with a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog. You can review or change your ad privacy preferences at any time inside the App under Settings → Privacy & Consent.
