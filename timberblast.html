Privacy Policy — Timber Blast 3D

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Package: com.timberblast3d.game

Timber Blast 3D ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play Timber Blast 3D on Android devices.

1. Local Game Data

All game progress — including unlocked levels, earned stars, high scores, in-game coins, power-ups, daily reward status, achievements, and audio/language settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a custom backend server and do not collect or sell your personal information.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP Consent)

Timber Blast 3D displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your region (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review or change your advertising privacy preferences at any time inside the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), diagnostic data, and coarse interaction signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games Services to view or submit scores to the Top Scores leaderboard, Google processes your Play Games profile and leaderboard scores under Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional and not required to play offline.

4. Children's Privacy

Timber Blast 3D is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact email tondeapel23@gmail.com, the developer via the support email listed on the Timber Blast 3D Google Play Store page.
