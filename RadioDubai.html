Privacy Policy — Radio UAE & Dubai Live

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  Package: com.luigy.radiouaedubai

This Privacy Policy describes how Radio UAE & Dubai Live ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you stream live FM and online radio stations from the United Arab Emirates on Android devices.

1. No Account or Personal Data Collection

Radio UAE & Dubai Live does not require user registration, accounts, passwords, names, or phone numbers. Your favorite radio stations, recently played history, equalizer/mixer presets, and language selection are saved strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

2. Live Radio Streaming & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App acts as an audio player connecting directly to publicly available third-party online radio streams from the UAE and international broadcasters. When you tune into a station, your device connects directly to that broadcaster's streaming server, which receives your IP address and standard media player headers solely to deliver the audio stream.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep Radio UAE & Dubai Live 100% free, the App displays banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on advertisements, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) and device/app identifiers
  • IP address (to estimate general coarse location such as country/region)
  • Ad interaction metrics, diagnostics, and crash logs

Learn more at Google Advertising & Privacy Policies.

4. GDPR / EEA & UK Consent (Google UMP)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App integrates the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. You may review, modify, or withdraw your advertising consent preferences at any time inside the App under Settings → Ad Privacy & GDPR Consent.

5. Children's Privacy

Radio UAE & Dubai Live is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Deletion

Because all app preferences (favorites, history, audio settings) are stored locally on your device, you can clear listening history inside Settings → Clear History or remove all local data by clearing App Storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.
