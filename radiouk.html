Privacy Policy

Radio UK & Ireland Live (com.radioukireland.live) • Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Radio UK & Ireland Live ("the App") handles information when you listen to live British and Irish online radio broadcasts on Android devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, Google Play User Data Policy, and Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform (UMP) requirements.

1. No Account & No Personal Data Collection

The App does not require registration, login, phone number, or email address to use. We do not collect, store, or sell your name, contacts, photos, location, microphone audio, or personal files.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your favorite radio stations, recently played station history, 5-band Studio Mixer / Equalizer presets, bass & surround preferences, and language selection are stored exclusively on your device using Android DataStore Preferences. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Radio UK & Ireland Live is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob (banner ads placed at the bottom of navigation screens and frequency-capped interstitial ads between natural station switches).

  • Google AdMob and its certified partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and basic app interaction diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • For users in the United Kingdom, Ireland, European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland, and applicable US states, the App integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to request your consent before personalized advertising is served.
  • You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time directly inside the App via Settings → Ad Privacy Preferences.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Live Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

When you play a radio station, your device connects directly to the broadcaster's publicly available audio stream URL. The broadcaster's server may receive standard network metadata (such as your IP address and user-agent string) strictly necessary to deliver the audio stream. All station names, streams, and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

5. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream live UK and Irish radio stations and load ads.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to keep live radio audio playing smoothly in the background with media controls in the notification shade and lock screen.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Prevents audio dropouts during background radio playback.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Displays playback controls while listening.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal or technical updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
