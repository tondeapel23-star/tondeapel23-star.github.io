This Privacy Policy explains how Snake Evolution handles information when you play our mobile game on Android devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping data handling transparent and minimal.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Snake Evolution does not require user accounts, registration, or login. We do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, home address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Local Game Progress Storage

Your unlocked levels, stars, high scores, career rank, coins, unlocked skins, missions, daily rewards, and settings (language, speed, board theme, sound, haptics) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. You can reset your local progress at any time inside the game's Settings menu or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) & Consent (GDPR / UMP)

Snake Evolution displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (banner ads in menus, occasional interstitial ads between levels, and optional rewarded video ads chosen by the player).

  • Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AD_ID), IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in ads.
  • For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Snake Evolution uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before displaying personalized ads.
  • You can review or change your ad privacy preferences at any time via Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent (GDPR) inside the app, or manage ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

Any network communication performed by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Local progress remains strictly on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Snake Evolution is intended for a general audience (13+) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
