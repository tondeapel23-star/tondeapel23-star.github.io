Privacy Policy — Parking Jam Escape

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  |  Package: com.parkingjam.escape

This Privacy Policy explains how Parking Jam Escape ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile game on Android devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and operating with an offline-first, privacy-respecting design.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Parking Jam Escape does not require user registration, accounts, login credentials, names, email addresses, or phone numbers. All game progress (completed levels, stars, in-game points, garage cosmetics, city buildings, and language preferences) is stored strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.

2. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep the game 100% free to play, Parking Jam Escape displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data automatically in order to serve, measure, and prevent fraud on advertisements, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) and device/app identifiers
  • IP address (used to infer general coarse location such as country/region)
  • Ad interaction data, crash logs, and diagnostics related to ad delivery

For more details on how Google handles data in its advertising products, please review Google Advertising & Privacy Policies.

3. GDPR / EEA & UK Consent Management (Google UMP)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App integrates the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. Before requesting personalized ads, you are presented with a consent dialog allowing you to accept, manage, or decline personalized advertising. You may review or change your privacy and ad consent preferences at any time inside the App via Settings → Ad Privacy & GDPR Consent.

4. Children's Privacy

Parking Jam Escape is designed for a general audience (teens and adults) and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress is stored solely on your device, you can erase all local game data at any time by using Settings → Reset All Progress inside the App, clearing the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the App.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to the App or changes in legal requirements. The latest version will always be published at this URL with the updated effective date.
