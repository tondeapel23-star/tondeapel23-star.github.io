Arrow Escape

Official Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 2026 • 100% Google Play & GDPR Compliant

Privacy First Summary: Arrow Escape does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data (such as names, emails, addresses, contacts, location, photos, or files). The game runs completely offline with local storage for your puzzle progress, and utilizes certified Google AdMob and Google UMP for compliant advertising.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Arrow Escape is designed with privacy by default. We do not require you to register, create an account, or grant sensitive device permissions. Specifically, we do NOT collect or request access to:

  • No Personal Identifiers: No name, email, phone number, or physical address.
  • No Location Data: Zero GPS, network coarse, or fine location access.
  • No Camera or Microphone: No audio or video capture permissions.
  • No Contacts or Media Files: No access to your phonebook, photos, or documents.
  • No Foreground Services: The game does not run any background or foreground tracking services.

2. Local Device Storage (Jetpack DataStore)

All your gameplay data is stored exclusively on your physical device using Android Jetpack DataStore:

  • Completed levels (1 to 500 across all 8 stages)
  • Virtual earned coins and daily bonus claim status
  • Equipped cosmetic arrow trail themes
  • Audio settings (Sound Effects on/off, Haptic Vibration on/off)

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Arrow Escape incorporates industry-standard SDKs provided by Google LLC to support ongoing development while keeping the game 100% free:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Displays non-intrusive banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between stages, and optional user-rewarded video ads to obtain free hints or extra coins. Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the standard Android Advertising ID - AD_ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK): Provides transparent consent management compliant with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK regulations. Players can review and adjust their privacy and consent choices at any time directly via the in-game Settings menu.

You can review Google's official privacy practices here: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Android Permissions Transparency

In full compliance with Google Play Store policies, Arrow Escape requests only standard, non-sensitive install-time permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Needed to load ad creatives and privacy consent options.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to gracefully detect offline status and disable ad requests when disconnected.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during arrow taps and stage completion (can be toggled off in Settings).
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Standard Google Play Services identifier for fraud prevention and ad frequency capping.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Escape is a family-friendly brain puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Advertising served complies with Google Play Designed for Families and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) guidelines.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain alignment with Google Play developer guidelines and applicable global data privacy regulations. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
