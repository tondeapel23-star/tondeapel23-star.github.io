Privacy Policy — PodAtlas: World Podcasts

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 • Package: com.podatlas.world

1. Overview

PodAtlas ("the App") is an independent RSS podcast player that lets you discover, follow, stream, and download publicly available podcast feeds from around the world. We respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data required to operate the App.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your followed podcasts, playback history, episode queue, offline audio downloads, and app preferences (language, country, speed, audio enhancements) are stored locally on your device. We do not require account registration and do not upload your personal listening history to our servers.

3. Direct RSS Audio Streaming & Directory Lookups

When you browse or search podcasts, the App queries public podcast directory endpoints. When you stream or download an episode, your device connects directly to the podcast publisher's official RSS server. As with any web or RSS client, the publisher's server may receive your IP address and standard HTTP User-Agent header in accordance with that publisher's own privacy policy. PodAtlas does not re-host, proxy, or alter publisher audio.

4. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob on browse screens. In regions where required (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized or non-personalized ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. You can manage your ad consent choices anytime from Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent.

Learn more about how Google uses information: Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Children's Privacy

PodAtlas is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Copyright, RSS Transparency & Takedown Requests

All podcast titles, descriptions, artwork, and audio remain the property of their respective creators and publishers. If you are a copyright holder or podcast publisher and wish to update or remove a feed listing from directory results, please contact us at tondeapel23@gmail.com with the feed URL and proof of ownership. Verified removal requests are processed promptly.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email tondeapel23@gmail.com.
