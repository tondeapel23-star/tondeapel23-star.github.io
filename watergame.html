Privacy Policy — Water Sort Evolution Pro

Package Name: com.watersort.evolution  |  Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Thank you for playing Water Sort Evolution Pro. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use our offline-first Android puzzle game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Water Sort Evolution Pro does not require user registration, login, or an account. We do not operate any external game servers and we do not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

2. Local Game Data (Stored Only on Your Device)

All game progress — including unlocked levels, star ratings, coins, diamonds, skins, daily streak, achievements, local leaderboard scores, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using Android DataStore Preferences. You can clear this data at any time via your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob (Third-Party Service)

Water Sort Evolution Pro is free to play and displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), including menu banners, infrequent interstitial ads between levels, and strictly optional rewarded video ads.

  • Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location (derived from IP address), and diagnostic/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage your GDPR advertising consent before displaying personalized ads.
  • You can review or change your ad consent preferences at any time inside the app via Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data in advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Children's Privacy

Water Sort Evolution Pro is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security & Offline Operation

Core gameplay works 100% offline. Internet access is used solely to load optional advertisements and consent forms via Google Play Services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available at this URL with an updated effective date.
