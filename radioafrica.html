Privacy Policy for Radio Africa Live

Last updated & effective date: November 16, 2026

Summary: Radio Africa Live does not require user registration, does not collect personal identity details (such as names, emails, addresses, contacts, or financial details), and does not track your precise GPS location. The app is dedicated strictly to non-political African music, culture, and arts radio streaming.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Radio Africa Live ("we", "our", or "the app") treats user information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

By installing and using Radio Africa Live, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute user privacy. Our application:

  • Does NOT require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Does NOT collect or store your name, telephone number, email, or physical address.
  • Does NOT access your contact list, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • Does NOT access or collect precise GPS location data.
  • Does NOT process financial, credit card, or payment information.

3. Information Collected Automatically & Third-Party SDKs

To provide high-quality audio streaming, display advertisements, and maintain stability, the app integrates standard third-party software kits:

A. Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Radio Africa Live uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical information in compliance with Google's policies, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • Device identifiers (device model, operating system version, carrier).
  • Coarse geographic data (country/city level determined from IP address).
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, diagnostic data).

Users can manage or reset their Advertising ID and opt out of personalized advertisements at any time through their Android device settings under: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete / Reset Advertising ID.

For more details, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

B. Public Radio Audio Streams

When you play a radio station, your device establishes a direct connection to the respective broadcaster's audio server. Broadcasters may log connection IP addresses to deliver the stream.

4. Android Device Permissions & Usage

  • INTERNET: Required to stream online audio broadcasts and load advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify network connectivity status to handle buffering smoothly.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to allow background playback when using other apps or when the screen is locked.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used to prevent audio stuttering when the screen turns off and keep the screen active in Car Kit mode.

5. Content Nature & Neutrality Policy

Radio Africa Live is strictly an entertainment and cultural platform showcasing African music genres (Afrobeats, Amapiano, Highlife, African Jazz, Gospel & Praise, Soukous, Reggae). The app does not produce or promote political campaigning or partisan commentary.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Radio Africa Live is a general audience entertainment application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

7. European GDPR & California CCPA Rights

Users can exercise privacy preferences regarding advertising identifiers directly through Android operating system settings.
